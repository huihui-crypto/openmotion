# Skill cover attribution

The Skills-page covers are OpenMotion samples rendered with HyperFrames from hand-written compositions with invented brands and content.

The presenter characters in the talking-head and editing covers use the **Micah** avatar style ("Avatar Illustration System") by Micah Lanier, generated with [DiceBear](https://www.dicebear.com/styles/micah/) 9.x and licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: colours were set per cover, and mouth and eye variants were switched over time to animate speech and blinking.

Covers using the Micah presenter: bilingual-captions, caption-styling, content-cover, doc-promo, edit-to-script, editorial-explainer, editorial-webinar, electric-impact-type, highlighter-notes, livestream-clips, monochrome-magazine, motion-graphic-placement, multicam-edit, orange-unfiltered, red-pen-archive, runway-rebel, shortlist-shorts, spec-sheet, talking-head-recreation, whiteboard-marker, wireframe-style.
