## Apply a recipe

1. Confirm the job, the material and the target format (duration, aspect ratio, language). Ask only for inputs the recipe marks as required.
2. Build through the installed OpenMotion production Skill: create or open the project, author the composition, save with revision protection and preview. This library Skill adds creative direction only; it registers no MCP Tool and no production route.
3. Treat every palette, font and timing value in a recipe as a starting point. A supplied Brandkit or the user's brand always wins over the recipe palette; keep the recipe's structure and motion rules.
4. Generated images, voice or music go through `openmotion_media_use` with configured providers. Show the cost or provider choice before paid generation. Never invent quotes, numbers, specs or dates; on-screen facts come from the user's material or a cited source.
5. Preview at normal speed with sound. Check every recipe item under **Review** before delivering, and say which checks were done.

Presenter recipes need the creator's own recording and measured speech timing. Do not place graphics over someone else's speech, and keep the face and mouth clear.

## Status and provenance

All eleven are **recipes / drafts**: written direction, not verified templates. No recipe ships an executable project or rendered comparison yet. When a recipe is used for a reviewed video, follow `openmotion-video-to-template` to package it with real before/after evidence.

The recipes were written for OpenMotion after surveying publicly listed workflow names and descriptions in ChatCut's preset gallery (November 2026). They are original text; no ChatCut prompts, source code, templates or media are included. Text follows the repository's Apache-2.0 license.
